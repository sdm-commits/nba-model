+30% more value: 630.7 ÷ 483.4 = 1.305. The model's top-10 picks produced 30% more career VORP than the actual top-10 picks across 8 drafts, and beat them in 7 of the 8.

15% bust rate: a bust is negative career VORP. 12 of the model's 80 picks finished below zero, against 25 of the 80 actual top-10 picks (31%).

28 players found: 28 of the model's top-10 picks were drafted outside the actual top 10 and went on to positive career VORP.

Not just box-score value: VORP is built from box-score stats, the same kind of numbers the model reads. Methodology ↓

Consensus boards: Rookie Scale consensus (2021–22), Hoops Prospects final boards (2019–20), and averages of three or four final public big boards from SI, CBS Sports, Bleacher Report, ESPN/DraftExpress, Yahoo and the Washington Post (2015–18). International and G League players were removed so the comparison is college-only.

Blended rank is w × model_rank + (1−w) × consensus_rank within each year's college first-round picks; a player missing from the consensus board gets rank 40. The top 10 by blended rank are kept and their career VORP summed.

Every weight from 0–100% was tested. The best weight is chosen on these same eight drafts, so the small blend edge is an upper bound.

Under the Hood

How it works

Data & Features

Trained on Torvik advanced college stats (2010–2026) supplemented with international Kaggle datasets. 150 engineered features span shooting, creation, defense, trajectory, team context, and positional archetypes inspired by EuroLeague clustering research. The model learns to predict career NBA impact: each season's DARKO rating above replacement level (−2.0), summed over the career. It is still judged on career VORP. Upperclassmen are discounted (juniors 6%, seniors 18%) because their college production is closer to their ceiling, except seniors who were elite producers (college BPM of 12 or more).

Validation

Walk-forward — for each draft year, the model trains only on prior years. No future data ever enters a prediction. Every configuration is scored on all eight draft classes (2015–2022), averaged over three training runs with different random seeds.

Position System

Continuous probabilistic position classification with post-prediction calibration. Point-forward detection prevents tall playmakers from being penalized as traditional bigs.

Known Limitations

No access to film, interviews, medicals, or combine data. Can't evaluate character, coachability, or team fit — real edges that GMs have and the model doesn't. Only evaluates college players, meaning it competes against GM boards that also include international and G League prospects.

Autonomous Optimizer

418 iterations. No human in the loop.

A hill-climbing loop runs the full model pipeline over and over. Each iteration mutates one to three settings: switching a feature on or off, or nudging a threshold, position adjustment, or model parameter. It retrains the model draft by draft and scores the result on the same yardstick as above: total career VORP of the model's top 10 across 2015–2022.

A change survives only if it adds at least 2 career VORP on the three-run average and helps in at least as many drafts as it hurts. Everything else is discarded. Of 418 candidates, 5 survived.
